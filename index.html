⚠ RISK RULES — READ BEFORE ENTERING
POSITION SIZE
Risk 5–10% of your account per trade. Never go all-in. Protect your capital first — no trade is worth blowing your account over.
STOP LOSS
If the contract loses 50% of the premium paid, exit the full position immediately. No averaging down. No hoping. Cut it and move on to the next setup.
TAKE PROFIT
Target 100% gain on the contract. At 100%, sell half your position and let the remaining contracts ride as free runners with zero risk.
DO NOT SELL EARLY 🔒
Hold until the stock hits your targeted price level. Selling early is the #1 account killer. Trust your levels, trust your gameplan. If your stop isn't hit and your target isn't reached — stay in the trade.
SQUEEZE PATIENCE ⏳
If in a higher timeframe squeeze, the breakout may not happen in the first half of the day. Be patient. Trust the setup. The 🔥 fire sale is your discount — wait for the market to catch up.

🔥 THE SQUEEZE BELIEF SYSTEM
As naked option traders we want volatility — dynamic price action so our premiums can move quickly.

When there's a squeeze, Market Makers are in a state of flux trying to price contracts appropriately. We can take advantage — buying during a squeeze at a discounted price and waiting for the breakout direction to see profits soar as Market Makers inflate price in the direction of demand.

🔥 Think of a squeeze as a limited-time clearance sale.

You buy at a discount. You sell at a premium. Bigger squeeze = bigger potential ROI. That's why squeezes = opportunity.

📊 90% of the time, we WILL hit our expected move. Trust the math, trust the system.

⏳ If we are in a higher timeframe squeeze, we may not break out in the first half of the day — be patient and trust the gameplan. The setup is still valid. Don't panic out early.
